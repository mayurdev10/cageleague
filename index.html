Need Answers?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I register a team for the upcoming season? ▼

Click on the "Register Your Team" button to open our team entry form. Complete your roster details and select your age division. Our tournament director will reach out within 24 hours to confirm your spot.

Are individual player walk-ins permitted for batting cages? ▼

Yes! Available cages can be reserved on a first-come, first-served basis or booked online. Check our live "Cage Status" monitor on this page to view real-time availability.

How does the live radar speed tracking work? ▼

All 8 championship bays feature integrated radar sensors that measure pitch velocity and hit exit velocity. Real-time metrics display instantly on the cage monitors and log into your player stats profile.
